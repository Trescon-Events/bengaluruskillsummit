About Bengaluru Skill Summit

The Bengaluru Skill Summit is a flagship initiative organised by the Government of Karnataka, led by the Department of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship & Livelihood (SDEL). Conceived as Karnataka’s premier platform for workforce development, the summit brings together leaders from government, industry, academia, global institutions, training providers, and youth to shape the future of India’s skills ecosystem.

Launched in November 2025, the inaugural edition was held at The Lalit Ashok and marked a significant milestone in advancing dialogue around reskilling, upskilling, digital transformation, and employability in emerging sectors. The summit established itself as a collaborative platform to showcase Karnataka’s achievements, share best practices, and foster cross-sectoral and inter-institutional partnerships.

Building on the strong foundation of its launch edition, the second edition of Bengaluru Skill Summit will take place in November 2026, further strengthening its role as a landmark event driving actionable strategies for workforce readiness, industry alignment, and sustainable economic growth.

The summit continues to serve as a catalyst for innovation, policy dialogue, and meaningful collaboration, positioning Karnataka at the forefront of skill development and future workforce transformation.

Workforce 2030: Scale, Systems, Synergy

The theme underscores the need to prepare for the future of work by expanding access (scale), strengthening institutional and delivery frameworks (systems), and promoting collaboration across sectors and stakeholders (synergy).

Skill Development Initiative

Launched under the Department of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship & Livelihood (SDEL), the Chief Minister’s Kaushalya Karnataka Yojane is the state’s flagship programme to harness its demographic dividend and prepare youth for the future of work. It focuses on short-term training, upskilling & reskilling, and entrepreneurship support across sectors, ensuring employability for those outside formal education and empowering the informal workforce. By aligning with the National Skill Development Policy and fostering industry–academia partnerships, the initiative drives inclusive growth and sustainable livelihoods. Its vision resonates strongly with the Bengaluru Skill Summit, where innovation, recognition, and collaboration in skilling are celebrated through platforms like Skillathon and the Kaushalya Karnataka Awards.

The Bengaluru Skill Summit 2026 builds on a strong foundation to shape a future where skills drive growth, inclusion, and innovation.

Vision & Impact

The Bengaluru Skill Summit 2026 is a movement to empower India’s workforce. By creating a platform for dialogue, innovation, and partnership, the summit aims to:

  • Provide a premier platform for workforce development and skilling innovation.
  • Showcase innovations, global best practices, and scalable models.
  • Drive policy recommendations and white papers.
  • Engage youth through the Skillathon competition and mentoring.
  • Recognise excellence in skilling through the Kaushalya Karnataka Awards.
  • Facilitate B2B, B2G, and G2G networking.
  • Strengthen India’s global positioning as a hub for skilled talent.
  • Enable industries to access future-ready talent pools.
  • Foster public–private partnerships for sustainable skilling initiatives.

The Bengaluru Skill Summit 2026 will set the stage for a future where skills fuel growth, inclusion, and innovation.
